Type I (K-Selection)

Low mortality throughout life, most individuals survive to old age. Few, high-investment offspring. Ex: humans, elephants, large mammals.

Starts with: High Intelligence, unlocks brain-based traits first.

Type II (Constant Mortality)

Mortality rate is roughly equal at all life stages, lanced reproduction. Ex: birds, rodents, many reptiles.

Starts with: Balanced spread & intelligence, most adaptable tree.

Type III (r-Selection)

Catastrophically high early mortality, only a tiny fraction survive to reproduce. Vast numbers of offspring. Ex: fish, insects, most invertebrates.

Starts with: Rapid spread, quantity over quality.
